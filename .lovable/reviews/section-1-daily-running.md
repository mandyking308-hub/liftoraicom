# Section 1 – Daily running: 13 tools

Record counts come from your live data, checked today.

## Keep: these are the core of your day
| Tool | What it does | Live records |
|---|---|---|
| **Approvals** | Everything paused or waiting for your yes/no | 21 items |
| **Work queue** | Every open task across businesses, with tabs for Today, Overdue, Blocked and By business. The Kindnesss 101-action tracker lives here | 133 items |
| **Alerts** | Warnings, incidents and escalations, most serious first | 11 alerts |
| **Decisions** | Decisions you've made and whether they were followed through | 10 decisions |

## Combine: they repeat each other
| Tools | Why combine | Live records |
|---|---|---|
| **Priority** + **Attention guard** → a tab in Work queue | Both rank "what needs you first", and Work queue already has a Today tab | 151 scores / 12 focus items |
| **Hand-offs & deadlines** → a tab in Work queue | Work queue already shows overdue items | 4 hand-offs |
| **Daily summaries** + **Weekly review** + **Reports** + **Reporting truth** → one "Reviews & reports" page | They're four views of the same thing: how the businesses did | 1 daily run, 1 weekly run, 0 reports, 7 number conflicts |

## Move to a better area
| Tool | Move to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Strategy** | AI & intelligence | Older growth-ideas engine, not a daily task | 13 insights |
| **Scheduling** | Customers | Customer bookings and appointments | 4 bookings |
