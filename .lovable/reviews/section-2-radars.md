# Section 2 – Radars & prospecting: 9 tools

Record counts come from your live data, checked today.

## Keep
| Tool | What it does | Live data |
|---|---|---|
| **CRM** | The shared contact book. Each business keeps its own qualification and suppression list | 137 contacts, 99 business links (30 Kindnesss) |
| **Relationship intelligence** | Your personal network memory: who you know, how much you trust them, and your next action | 1,230 people, 1,363 history entries |
| **Billionaire intelligence** | Research-only coverage of billionaires and the routes to reach them | 2,754 people, about 4,100 routes, 3,428 wealth snapshots |
| **Funding radar** | Recently funded companies that might need a build | 27 companies, 9 shortlisted, 10 investors (one monthly run so far) |

## Combine
| Tools | Into | Why | Live data |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strategic organisations** | Relationship intelligence (as a tab) | Both are your network register: one covers people, the other organisations | 53 organisations |
| **Relationship health** | Relationship intelligence (as a tab) | It scores the same relationships | 5 scores |
| **Duplicate people** | CRM (as a tab) | Finding duplicate contacts is housekeeping for the CRM | 4 profiles, 1 duplicate candidate |

## Move to Older versions for now (kept, and can come back)
| Tool | Why | Live data |
|---|---|---|
| **PR radar** | Built but never used: no media opportunities, journalists or coverage recorded | 12 sources set up, everything else 0 |
| **Distressed radar** | Built but empty | 0 opportunities, 0 assets |

**Result:** 9 tools become **4 boxes**: CRM, Relationship intelligence, Billionaire intelligence and Funding radar.
Nothing is deleted. PR radar and Distressed radar stay one click away under Older versions and can be brought back the day you want to use them.
