# Section 15 — Schools & private (3 tools)

Founder-only and private: nothing here is used in campaigns, Apollo or outreach.

| Tool | What it does | Live data |
|---|---|---|
| Admissions tracker | Deadlines, tests and status per school | 66 schools with admissions details (incl. JAGS) |
| School visits | Your visit notes and assessments | 70 school records, 1 visit template |
| School Register (GIAS) | Search the official DfE school register | 52,591 schools from 1 approved import |

Admissions and visits use the same 70 school records; admissions is stored inside each one.

## Recommendation: keep all 3 boxes, nothing combined
They're small, private and each does a different job. Combining Admissions into Visits would save one box but hide deadlines one click deeper, so I wouldn't.
