# Giving Rail — launch brand system

## Brand position

**Name:** Giving Rail  
**Tagline:** Giving that moves.  
**Descriptor:** Business giving. Charity control. Proof built in.

Giving Rail is positioned as infrastructure rather than a fundraising campaign brand. The product sits between a business promise and a charity outcome: defining the commitment, controlling the relationship, moving the contribution through an approved route, and preserving evidence.

## Voice

- Clear, calm and operational.
- Avoid sentimental charity clichés.
- Do not imply that a draft campaign is legally approved.
- Do not call an organisation, project or campaign "verified" without stating what was checked.
- Prefer plain language: promise, agreement, payment, evidence, project, contribution.
- Avoid claiming a global legal solution. Use "country pack" / "jurisdiction-gated" language.

## Visual system

Primary ink: `#12342f`  
Secondary green: `#1d4b43`  
Warm cream: `#fbf8f1`  
Warm accent: `#e99b56`  
Soft sage: `#dce9df`

Display type: DM Serif Display.  
Interface type: DM Sans.

The symbol combines two rail lines with the existing giving / partnership motif. The rail is a metaphor for controlled movement and repeatable infrastructure, not transport.

## Core messages

1. **Giving that moves.**
2. **A better route from promise to impact.**
3. **Charge for useful infrastructure. Keep the charitable amount visible.**
4. **Direct giving stays simple. Sales-linked giving gets the controls it needs.**
5. **Global by design. Local by rule.**
