# Mandy's Founder User Guide

Plain-English operating guide for Liftor. Founder-only. Mirrors `/founder/user-guide`.

## What Liftor is
Liftor is your private founder operating system. It helps you design, dry-run, monitor and decide on businesses you run, without exposing anything externally until you say so.

## What Liftor is not
- Not a public marketing platform.
- Not an auto-sender (no emails/SMS/social fire on their own).
- Not an M&A firm. Not a legal/tax/clinical decision engine.

## What to open first each morning
1. `/founder/start-here` — guided 10-step path
2. `/founder/command-centre` — alerts, attention items, lifecycle
3. `/founder/runtime-mode` — confirm Simulation / founder-only

## How to add a business
Use the wizard at `/founder/start-here/setup-business`. Always saves as **draft / not_live**.

## How to run the setup wizard
Six steps: shell → basics → evidence → dry-run readiness → confirm & save → next steps. Saving requires typing `FOUNDER APPROVED`.

## How to use Command Centre
Read attention panels top to bottom. Click amber/red items first.

## How to use the lifecycle sidebar
Shows each business's stage and what's blocking it.

## How to check safety gates
- `/founder/runtime-mode`
- `/founder/approvals-ops`
- `/founder/business-activation`

## How to use Finance Hub
Revenue Autopilot, Quote-to-Cash, treasury, cashflow forecasts — all review-first.

## How to use Marketing Hub
Campaign Factory, social autopilot (drafts only), longform, conversion assets. Nothing sends without founder approval.

## How to use Healthcare Overlay safely
Remains **NOT LIVE / BLOCKED** by default. Use for internal readiness only.

## How to use the Data Room safely
**Closed by default.** No external tokens issued. Internal review only.

## How to use Buyer Warm-Up
Quiet tracking only. Outbound contact is hard-blocked unless `founder_approved_to_contact = true`.

## How to ask the AI Co-Pilot
Open `/founder/copilot`. Ask plain questions like:
- What should I do first?
- Which business needs setup?
- What is missing before launch?
- Is anything external live?
- Which businesses are due for sale review?

## What not to switch on without a founder decision
External sending, customer/investor/adviser/buyer portals, healthcare go-live, provider activations, cron jobs, public exposure of `/founder/*`.

## Daily 15-minute routine
Start Here → Command Centre attention → Approvals Ops → one business lifecycle → one Co-Pilot question.

## Weekly review routine
Business Weekly Review per business → portfolio priority → funding shortlist → buyer warm-up review.

## If something looks wrong
Stop. Confirm Simulation mode. Take a snapshot via `/founder/recovery`. Ask Co-Pilot "what is blocked right now?". Do not flip anything live.
