# Aurelia — User Guide

_Canonical source: GitHub `docs/business-manuals/aurelia/user-guide.md`. The in-app Business Manuals screen renders this exact file._

## What this business does
Aurelia sells creative digital learning to schools and education groups, with safeguarding and data handling built in. The positioning is **Create. Learn. Achieve. Safely.** The people who buy are digital learning leads, IT and technology directors, safeguarding leads and the education leaders above them.

## How to use it in Liftor
1. Open **Founder → Education Commercial Layer** and select **Aurelia**.
2. Review the relevance list — digital, technology, safeguarding and innovation roles score highest.
3. Pick the contacts you want. Aurelia's shell is prepared but not launched, so this is preparation only.
4. Claim ownership only if you genuinely intend Aurelia to be the first approach for that person.
5. Leave the campaign non-live until you decide otherwise.

## Dashboards and statuses
- **Relevance score** — fit for Aurelia, 0 to 100.
- **Eligible** — every safety check passed.
- **Owner brand** — which portfolio brand holds outbound.
- **Campaign state** — `prepared_not_live`.
- **Send blocked** — blocked, by design.

## Choosing contacts
Digital learning directors, heads of edtech, IT and technology directors, designated safeguarding leads, and group leaders with a technology or transformation remit. Skip nurseries.

## Approving a campaign
Not required today. Aurelia is prepared, not launched. When the time comes, approval is deliberate, recorded, and still not the same as sending.

## What not to do
- Do not launch Aurelia ahead of Billy — the controlled first campaign is Billy's.
- Do not claim a safeguarding certification or accreditation Aurelia does not hold.
- Do not duplicate a contact to give Aurelia its own copy — use the relationship layer.
- Do not override a reply, unsubscribe, suppression or bounce block.
