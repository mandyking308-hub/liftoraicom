# Aurelia — Create. Learn. Achieve. Safely.

## The problem
The digital tools students find most engaging are often the ones safeguarding leads are least comfortable approving. Schools end up choosing between creative ambition and a clean safeguarding position — and creative ambition usually loses.

## What Aurelia is
Creative digital learning designed so that safety is part of the product, not an afterthought. Aurelia gives students genuine creative range while giving your safeguarding and technology leads a clear, explainable position on data, access and control.

## Who it is for
- Digital learning and edtech leads
- IT and technology directors
- Designated safeguarding leads
- Curriculum, innovation and transformation leaders
- Education groups and trusts standardising across many settings

## Benefits
- **Creative without compromise.** Students design, build and present real work.
- **Safeguarding-first.** Clear data handling, clear access controls, clear answers for governors.
- **Explainable.** A written safeguarding and data summary you can circulate before any product conversation.
- **Consistent across a group.** Standardise once, apply across settings.

## Use cases
- Creative and design work across the curriculum
- Digital literacy and safe online practice
- Cross-setting standardisation for trusts and groups
- Enrichment and extended project work

## Getting started
1. **Screen it.** We send the safeguarding and data-handling summary first.
2. **Overview.** A short session for your digital and safeguarding leads together.
3. **Pilot.** One setting or one year group.
4. **Roll out.** Agree scope across your group with an implementation timeline.

## Support
A named contact takes you from first summary through pilot to rollout, with practical onboarding for teaching and technology staff. To start, reply to your contact at Aurelia and ask for the safeguarding and data summary.
