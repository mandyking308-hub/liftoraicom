# Kingsbridge Global — User Guide

_Canonical source: GitHub `docs/business-manuals/kingsbridge-global/user-guide.md`. The in-app Business Manuals screen renders this exact file._

## What this business does
Kingsbridge Global works with education groups on international provision and cross-border partnerships — **Education Without Borders.** The buyers are group and international leaders, partnership and development directors, regional heads and commercial or procurement leaders inside large groups.

## How to use it in Liftor
1. Open **Founder → Education Commercial Layer** and select **Kingsbridge Global**.
2. Review the relevance list — international, global, regional, partnership and group-leadership roles score highest.
3. Note that senior group people often score for more than one brand. Read the prioritisation reason before claiming.
4. Claim ownership only where Kingsbridge is genuinely the right first approach.
5. Leave the campaign non-live.

## Dashboards and statuses
- **Relevance score** — fit for Kingsbridge Global.
- **Owner brand** — check before claiming; group leaders are contested.
- **Campaign state** — `prepared_not_live`.
- **Send blocked** — blocked, by design.

## Choosing contacts
Group directors, international directors, regional heads, partnership and development directors, and commercial or procurement leaders inside multi-setting groups. Skip nurseries and single small settings.

## Approving a campaign
Not required today. Kingsbridge Global is prepared, not launched.

## What not to do
- Do not approach a group leader another brand is already speaking to.
- Do not promise accreditation, curriculum recognition or regulatory status that has not been confirmed.
- Do not duplicate contacts across brands.
- Do not override a reply, unsubscribe, suppression or bounce block.
