# Kindnesss — Small acts. Big hearts.

## The problem
Every school wants a stronger culture of kindness and a wellbeing programme that actually runs. What stops it is rarely intent — it is time. Tutor time is short, staff are stretched, and most wellbeing material assumes a training day and a free timetable slot that do not exist.

## What Kindnesss is
Small, practical wellbeing and PSHE activities designed for the time you really have. Five-minute routines, kindness prompts and student-experience practices that a tutor can pick up and run the same morning, with no preparation.

## Who it is for
- Wellbeing and pastoral leads
- PSHE and character education coordinators
- Heads of year and student experience
- Inclusion teams with a wellbeing remit
- Primary, secondary and sixth-form settings, and the groups above them

## Benefits
- **Fits the time you have.** Built for five minutes, not fifty.
- **No preparation.** Tutors can use it cold.
- **Consistent language.** The same simple vocabulary across year groups.
- **Sits inside your programme.** Complements your existing PSHE and pastoral plan rather than replacing it.

## Use cases
- Tutor time and form-group routines
- Kindness weeks and whole-school themes
- Student voice and peer support sessions
- Transition and settling-in periods

## Getting started
1. **Sample pack.** We send a one-week pack so your pastoral team can try it.
2. **Try one week.** One year group, one form, or one tutor team.
3. **Agree scope.** Choose the settings and year groups to cover.
4. **Roll out.** Materials and usage guidance are supplied with every pack.

## Support
A named contact handles everything from sample to rollout, including practical questions from tutors. To start, reply to your contact at Kindnesss and ask for the one-week sample pack.

## An honest note
Kindnesss is a culture and routine resource. It is not a mental health service and is not a substitute for professional support where a child needs it.
