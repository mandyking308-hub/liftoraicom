# Kindnesss — User Guide

_Canonical source: GitHub `docs/business-manuals/kindnesss/user-guide.md`. The in-app Business Manuals screen renders this exact file._

## What this business does
Kindnesss provides short wellbeing and PSHE activities to schools — **Small acts. Big hearts.** Five-minute tutor-time routines and kindness prompts that need no training day. The buyers are wellbeing and pastoral leads, PSHE leads, heads of student experience and the leaders above them.

## How to use it in Liftor
1. Open **Founder → Education Commercial Layer** and select **Kindnesss**.
2. Review the relevance list — wellbeing, pastoral, PSHE and student-experience roles score highest.
3. Watch the owner column carefully. Kindnesss and Billy attract similar people, so Liftor will often show that Billy already owns a contact.
4. Choose contacts and claim ownership only where Kindnesss is genuinely the right first approach.
5. Leave the campaign non-live.

## Dashboards and statuses
- **Relevance score** — fit for Kindnesss.
- **Owner brand** — often Billy for shared wellbeing/inclusion contacts.
- **Campaign state** — `prepared_not_live`.
- **Send blocked** — blocked, by design.

## Choosing contacts
Wellbeing leads, pastoral leads, heads of year and student experience, PSHE coordinators, and inclusion staff where wellbeing is a stated part of the role. Skip nurseries.

## Approving a campaign
Not required today. Kindnesss is prepared, not launched.

## What not to do
- Do not approach a person Billy is already talking to just because Kindnesss also fits.
- Do not describe Kindnesss as a mental health service or clinical intervention.
- Do not duplicate contacts to split them between brands.
- Do not override a reply, unsubscribe, suppression or bounce block.
