# Billy and the Wild Forest — User Guide

_Canonical source: GitHub `docs/business-manuals/billy-and-the-wild-forest/user-guide.md`. The in-app Business Manuals screen renders this exact file._

## What this business does
Billy and the Wild Forest sells original illustrated story resources to schools and education groups. The strongest reason a school buys is SEN and inclusion: the books are written so children who struggle with emotional language can still take part, and the artwork keeps reluctant readers engaged. Secondary reasons are wellbeing, pastoral work and reading for pleasure.

## How to use it in Liftor
1. Open **Founder → Education Commercial Layer**.
2. Choose **Billy and the Wild Forest**.
3. Look at the relevance list. Every contact already in the CRM has been scored for Billy; high fit means SEN, inclusion, literacy or wellbeing responsibility.
4. Choose the people you want in the first batch — between 25 and 50, and only excellent ones.
5. Claim ownership for Billy. Liftor checks nobody else in the portfolio is already talking to that person.
6. Review the campaign shell and the three emails.
7. Approve the campaign only when you are ready. Approval alone still does not send.

## Dashboards and statuses
- **Relevance score** — how well the person fits Billy, 0 to 100.
- **Eligible** — every safety check passed for Billy specifically.
- **Owner brand** — which portfolio brand currently holds outbound to that person.
- **Campaign state** — `configured_not_live` today. It stays non-live until you change it.
- **Send blocked** — will show blocked until mailboxes and Smartlead mapping are ready.

## Choosing contacts
Prefer SENCos, inclusion leads, heads of learning support, literacy leads, and trust or group leaders with inclusion in their remit. Skip nurseries. Skip anyone showing a suppression, unsubscribe or bounce flag — Liftor will refuse them anyway.

## Approving a campaign
Approval is a deliberate act with your name on it. It records who approved, when and why. It does not start sending; sending needs the campaign live, mailboxes ready and Smartlead mapping ready as well.

## What not to do
- Do not create a second Billy record or a duplicate contact — one person exists once.
- Do not override a reply, unsubscribe, suppression or bounce block. Liftor will not let you, and that is intentional.
- Do not launch to hundreds of people. The first campaign is deliberately 25 to 50.
- Do not touch Neon Candy data from this screen.
