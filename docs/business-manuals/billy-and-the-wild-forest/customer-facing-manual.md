# Billy and the Wild Forest — For Schools and Education Groups

## The problem
Some children cannot easily find the words for what they feel. In a busy classroom that shows up as withdrawal, frustration or disengagement from reading altogether. Inclusion and pastoral teams know it, but the resources available are often either text-heavy, clinical in tone, or need training time nobody has.

## What Billy and the Wild Forest is
A collection of original illustrated stories built around emotional understanding and inclusion. Every title is hand-painted rather than stock-illustrated, and the text is written deliberately short and low-load so that children who find reading effortful can still take part. Each book comes with simple inclusion notes and talking points.

## Who it is for
- SENCos, inclusion leads and learning support teams
- Literacy and reading leads
- Pastoral and wellbeing staff
- Primary, secondary and sixth-form settings, and the education groups and trusts above them

## Benefits
- **Genuinely SEN-friendly.** Short sentences, generous white space, clear emotional cues, and no assumption of fluent reading.
- **Artwork children stay with.** Original illustration is the reason many reluctant readers keep the page open.
- **Ready to use.** A ten-minute session needs no preparation and no training day.
- **Fits what you already run.** Designed to sit beside your existing inclusion, PSHE and reading provision.

## Use cases
- One-to-one or small-group SEN sessions
- Reading-for-pleasure corners and library provision
- Tutor time and pastoral check-ins
- Transition support between year groups or settings
- Parent and family reading sessions

## Getting started
1. **Sample.** We send a sample title and the accompanying inclusion notes so your team can judge it against your own provision.
2. **Trial.** Choose one class, group or setting and try it for a few weeks.
3. **Roll out.** Agree the titles and quantities for your school or across your group.
4. **Settle in.** We supply the inclusion notes and usage guidance with every set.

## Support
Your named contact stays with you from sample through to rollout. Questions about titles, quantities, delivery or classroom use are answered by the same person. To start, reply to your contact at Billy and the Wild Forest and ask for the sample set.

## An honest note
We do not claim measured attainment gains. What we offer is well-made, inclusive, beautiful reading material that children want to open, and staff can use straight away.
