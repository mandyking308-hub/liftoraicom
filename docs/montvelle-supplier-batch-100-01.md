## Route standard

Every supplier in this batch has at least one official supplier route recorded. The minimum route is the supplier's own booking, enquiry, membership or access page. Published telephone numbers and email addresses are added where clearly available; no personal details or guessed email patterns are used.

Examples enriched with direct published phone/email in this pass include Ritz-Carlton reservations, St. Regis London reservations, EDITION London reservations, Bvlgari Hotel London, Park Hyatt London River Thames, Rocco Forte Hotels and XO private aviation.

## Concierge rule

Web booking/enquiry routes are fulfilment-ready where normal public booking is available. Private-club membership routes are retained but marked not fulfilment-ready until membership, guest or reciprocal eligibility is established.
